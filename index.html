How Candle Sync Works
1-Minute Canonical Offline Engine
1-Minute Candles: We download genuine 1-minute historical candles directly from Binance Futures REST API. No interpolated or synthetic base bars are used.
Deep Historical Sync: Sync downloads candles in batches of 1,500, moving backward through history from your oldest stored candle toward earlier available market data.
Sync All: Starts historical synchronization for both BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT, followed by a forward sync to catch up to the current minute.
Sync Again (Resumption & Deepening): Does not re-download already stored candles. It checks existing stored history and continues backward from your oldest candle (oldestTime - 1ms) to deepen historical coverage, and forward to capture recent candles.
Duplicate Protection: Existing candles are identified by their unique timestamp keys (symbol_tf_time). Stored candles are never duplicated; updates are idempotent.
Incremental Updates: Newly available candles are added without replacing or re-downloading the entire database.
Interrupted Sync Safety: Every 1,500-candle batch is committed immediately in its own database transaction. If interrupted, already downloaded batches remain saved, and the next sync resumes from stored boundaries.
Larger Timeframes: 3m, 5m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 2h, 4h, 6h, 12h, 1D, and 1W charts are mathematically synthesized on-the-fly directly from stored 1-minute candles.
Offline Charts: Previously downloaded candles remain stored locally and can be viewed and navigated without internet.
Historical Scrolling: Scrolling backward on the chart queries older candles from local IndexedDB storage first, aggregating them instantly.
Gap Repair: Repair Gaps checks for missing intervals (>60s) between stored candles and fetches only the missing candles from Binance.
Storage: Historical candles are stored permanently in IndexedDB until explicitly cleared by the application or user. In-memory render cache is bounded to 5,000 bars for RAM safety.
Internet Required: Downloading new historical data and repairing gaps requires an active internet connection. Offline browsing uses already stored data.
Sync Progress & Rate Limiting: The progress indicator shows live speed (candles/s) and current batch date range. Deep sync takes time because data is downloaded in batches with API rate-limit pacing.

Simulation Model & Truth Disclosure:
• Execution: Candle-based simulation (no tick data). Signals evaluate on closed candle; orders and closes fill at next open plus fixed slippage.
• Brackets: A stop and a target touched in the same candle resolve conservatively as a LOSS (Stop Loss wins).
• Funding & Liquidation: Constant 8h funding rate at UTC boundaries. Isolated margin liquidation with maintenance margin.
• Limitations: No order book depth or liquidity queues; no partial fills; liquidation uses an estimated maintenance margin rate; historical simulations are not predictions of future live profit.
